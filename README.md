# Hi, I'm Janis

I'm a father of two who works with production machinery and enjoys exploring AI, automation, and practical problem-solving.

## Building things with AI

I don't have formal software development education. Instead, I use AI to explore unfamiliar technologies, understand problems, and turn ideas into working applications.

I decide what I want to achieve, define requirements, question proposed solutions, test results, identify problems, and keep improving what I've built. ChatGPT and Codex help with the code, tests, and documentation. I'm learning through the process rather than pretending to know everything beforehand.

I'm especially curious about what someone with hands-on experience, persistence, and modern AI tools can accomplish outside their usual field.

## Things I'm building

**[Language Learning App](https://github.com/janisp123/language-learning-app)**  
Short lessons for practical Latvian, Russian and English, plus English → Japanese for my own learning. Includes pronunciation and offline practice, with no account or saved learner progress. Language and device review are still ongoing.  
[Try the app](https://janisp123.github.io/language-learning-app/learn/) · [About the project](https://janisp123.github.io/language-learning-app/)

**[Mental-health questionnaire](https://github.com/janisp123/mental-health-questionnaire)**  
A free, multilanguage tool to help adults organize what they want to discuss at a mental-health appointment. Answers stay on the user's device. It is an appointment-preparation aid, not a diagnostic or validated screening tool.  
[Open the questionnaire](https://janisp123.github.io/mental-health-questionnaire/questionnaire/) · [Read about it](https://janisp123.github.io/mental-health-questionnaire/)

**[Idle Game & Browser Game Engineering Library](https://github.com/janisp123/idle_game)**  
An early-stage hobby project with two goals: explore a browser-based idle RPG and build a public library of practical game-engineering knowledge. The research documentation is growing; the game itself is not implemented yet.  
[Browse the research library](https://github.com/janisp123/idle_game/blob/main/docs/RESEARCH_LIBRARY.md)

I build these because I enjoy experimenting and hope that something I make might be useful to someone else. Feedback and thoughtful suggestions are welcome.
